# SME Finance Copilot

An AI finance copilot for small businesses. It brings bills, payments and bank activity together into one picture, forecasts cash, warns about problems early, shows how the business is doing, recommends what to look at (such as supplier price rises or slow-paying customers), answers questions in plain English, and compares financing options when cash runs short. Personal and sensitive details never reach the AI.

Built for the International Track, Topic E: SME Finance Copilot.
