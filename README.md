# Cocent

Cocent is an AI finance copilot for small businesses. It brings bills, payments and bank activity together into one picture, forecasts cash, warns about problems early, shows how the business is doing, recommends what to look at (such as supplier price rises or slow-paying customers), answers questions in plain English, and compares financing options when cash runs short. Personal and sensitive details never reach the AI.

Built for the International Track, Topic E: SME Finance Copilot.

## The name

- **Co**: a copilot. It works alongside the owner and doesn't replace them.
- **Cent**: every cent counts. The cent is the small unit of the dollar, the euro and most other currencies, so the name works anywhere.
- **Sounds like "consent"**: nothing happens without a person's say-so. Every action has a fixed approval level, risky ones need two people, personal and bank details are hidden before the AI sees any text, and every step goes into a tamper-evident activity log.
